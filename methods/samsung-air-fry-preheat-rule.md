- **Source:** Joe, to Grok Bot, Sun Oct 11, 2026, 4:30 PM CDT. Oven Air Fry Chef stores it as a permanent rule.

Joe's words:

## Method
1. Set regular **Bake** to the target temperature to preheat. This is usually the same temperature as the Air Fry step.
2. When the oven reports it is preheated, cancel Bake.
3. Start **Air Fry** at the target temperature and cook for the time given.

## Related rule
Cook directly on the rack unless the food needs a pan or tray (Joe's rule 2 for Oven Air Fry Chef, Oct 11, 2026).

## Note
Samsung's own guidance says Air Fry needs no preheat (Oven Air Fry Chef research, Oct 11, 2026). Joe's rule overrides it because Air Fry works better in a hot oven.
