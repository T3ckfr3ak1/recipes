# Tyson chicken breast chunks in Samsung Air Fry

**From Oven Air Fry Chef, not yet tested by Joe.** Relayed Oct 25, 2026.

These are for raw Tyson chicken breast cut into chunks. R11 (`recipes/bdb4ed7a90b1.json`, Seasoned Chicken Breast) uses whole breasts, so its own times weren't changed.

## Settings
- Food: raw Tyson chicken breast chunks, about 1 inch, 1 lb.
- Pan: single layer on a lightly oiled rimmed sheet pan, rack position 3.
- Preheat: Bake 425F. When preheated, cancel Bake.
- Air Fry 425F for 12 min. Stir at 6 min.
- Done at 165F with no pink.

## Back to back with the roasted diced potatoes
Uses the potato steps in `meal-plan/2026-10-20_to_2026-11-04.md` (Air Fry 425F, 25 min).
1. Roast the potatoes first, 25 min.
2. Take the potatoes out and cook the chicken, 12 min.
3. Put the potatoes back in for the last 2 min to rewarm.

Oven Air Fry Chef's "tent the potatoes with foil" while the chicken cooks is **not possible**: Joe has no foil until he buys it. How to keep the potatoes warm without foil isn't specified.
