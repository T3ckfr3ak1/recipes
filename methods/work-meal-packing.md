# Work meal packing

**Source:** Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026.

- Work meals go in quart Ziploc freezer bags, never Tupperware.
- No foil (Joe has none until he buys it).
- At work: dump the meal out of the bag onto a paper plate, microwave it, and eat with plastic utensils.
- For burritos and wraps, lay a damp paper towel over them on the plate. The reheat times in the Pulled Pork Wrap (`recipes/57daf991b4f8.json`) and the Pork and Black Bean Burritos (`recipes/e48582ad361a.json`) are starting points, not tested by Joe. Check for 165F in the center.
