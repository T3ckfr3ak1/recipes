# Lesson: Instant Pot shredded chicken, Oct 25, 2026

**Source:** Joe's result via Meal Plan, Sun Oct 25, 2026, 4:06 PM (America/Chicago).

## What was cooked
- 3.5 lb **thawed** chicken breasts (the 2.5 lb plus the 1 lb Tyson), R7 (`recipes/16671902db4d.json`).
- 1 cup water with 1 1/2 tsp Better Than Bouillon, no trivet. On top: 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 3/4 tsp salt, 3/4 tsp pepper, 12 candied jalapeno slices, and a splash of jar syrup.
- Emeril Lagasse Pressure AirFryer Y8D-AF-36: POULTRY 12 min, 10 min natural release.

## Joe's result
- The driest, blandest, toughest pulled chicken he's seen.

## Lessons
- R7's 12 to 15 min is for **frozen** breasts. Don't use it for thawed breasts. The thawed time is TBD until it's researched.
- The flavor was too weak. The seasoning amounts need to go up, and the new amounts are TBD.

## Fix (Head Chef)
Quick release instead of a 10 min natural release, and 10 min for thawed breasts.

## Today's rescue (Head Chef's version, untested)
1. Add to the shredded chicken: 1 cup water, 1 tsp Better Than Bouillon, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp onion powder, 1/4 tsp salt, and 2 Tbsp candied jalapeno syrup.
2. Put the KitchenAid glass lid on (not the pressure lid). Press Saute, turn the dial to POULTRY, and heat only until it's steaming. Don't let it bubble. Stir in 2 Tbsp butter.
3. Press Cancel and leave it on Keep Warm with the glass lid for 20 min, stirring once halfway.
4. Do not pressure cook it again.
5. Bag every portion with broth.

Head Chef's version (via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026) replaces Meal Plan's earlier light-bubble step. Manual check (`appliance-manuals/emeril-lagasse-y8d-af-36.pdf`): the Unit goes to Keep Warm by itself only when a cooking cycle finishes. Pressing Cancel ends any function, Keep Warm included, and the Panel has a separate Keep Warm Button. After Cancel, check that the screen shows Keep Warm; if it doesn't, press the Keep Warm Button.

## Result of the rescue (Joe, 4:42 PM, Oct 25, 2026, via Head Chef)
Joe's words: "the chicken is doen now, i guess, it has flavor, its still fairly tough, but like you said, we cant uncook it".

The rescue (warming it in broth, with 2 Tbsp butter stirred in at the 10 min mark, as Head Chef reported) brought the flavor back, but the meat stayed fairly tough. You can add flavor back to overcooked breast, but you can't make it tender again, so the fix is to not overcook it in the first place: thawed breasts get POULTRY 10 min with a quick release (R7, `recipes/16671902db4d.json`).

Joe also said (4:43 PM, via Meal Plan): "maybe next time i suggest we slow cook, we slow cook". He had suggested slow cooking this chicken, and it was pressure cooked instead. See the cooking-method rule in `preferences/food-preferences.md`.
