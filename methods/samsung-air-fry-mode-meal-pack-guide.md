# How to use the Samsung Air Fry mode (meal pack guide)

- **Source:** Joe's Meal Pack, Oct 20 to Nov 4, 2026, Part 2 intro. Thrifty built the pack, and Meal Plan keeps it now.
- The pack says "Always preheat fully (wait for the beep)". Joe's own rule is to preheat with Bake and then switch to Air Fry; see `samsung-air-fry-preheat-rule.md`.

Copied from the pack:

> How to use the Samsung Air Fry mode (read once). It cooks somewhere between a real air fryer and regular baking, so these times are a starting point.
> - Always preheat fully (wait for the beep). Middle or upper-middle rack.
> - Use a lightly oiled sheet pan. A wire rack set on the pan (or the Samsung air fry tray) helps the bottoms crisp.
> - Single layer, space between pieces. Crowding = steaming, not crisping.
> - Tops brown faster than bottoms, so flip or stir halfway.
> - Start checking at the low end of the time. Go by the doneness cue and internal temp, not the clock.
>
> Safe internal temps: chicken 165°F (wings are better at 175 to 190°F), ground beef 160°F, pork for shredding 195 to 205°F, sausage 160°F, reheated leftovers 165°F.

Also from the pack (R6): "Use regular Bake mode for frozen pizza (Air Fry mode can burn the top before the crust is done)."
