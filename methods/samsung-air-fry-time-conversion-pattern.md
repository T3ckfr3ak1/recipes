# Samsung Air Fry time conversion pattern (derived, failed for pizza)

- **Source:** Oven Air Fry Chef, Sun Oct 11, 2026, from a research pass it ran.
- **Status:** derived, not verified by Joe. Joe's first test, a Red Baron Personal Pan Pizza, **burned** with this pattern. See `lesson-red-baron-personal-pan-pizza-2026-10-11.md`.

## The pattern (Oven Air Fry Chef memory, Oct 11, 2026)
> Samsung Air Fry pattern (derived from fries, tots, fish sticks, onion rings): Samsung Air Fry time ≈ 0.96 × package bake time midpoint (range 0.67–1.25), same temp as package.

## Data points it used (package bake vs. Samsung's Air Fry cooking guide)
| Food | Package bake | Samsung guide result | Time ratio (Samsung ÷ bake, midpoints) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen fries (Ore-Ida Golden Crinkles) | 425°F, 25–35 min for a full bag | 425–450°F, 25–30 min | 0.92 |
| Frozen tater tots (Ore-Ida) | 425°F, 20–25 min | 450°F, 20–25 min | 1.00 |
| Frozen fish sticks (Gorton's) | 425°F, about 15–17 min | 425°F, 18–22 min | 1.25 |
| Frozen onion rings (Ore-Ida Onion Ringers) | 400°F, 24–28 min | 400–425°F, 15–20 min | 0.67 |

The stored transcript cuts off the rest of the table, including a chicken nuggets row.

Weaknesses the bot listed:
- Samsung published no frozen-pizza data.
- Samsung's guide assumes the Air Fry tray, but Joe cooks on the bare rack.
- Samsung's guide batches are much larger than one pizza.

Sources: https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/874945/samsung-30-inch-freestanding-electric-smart-range-owner-manual.html?page=42 , http://directionsforme.org/product/8953 , https://www.directionsforme.org/product/8972 , https://www.gortons.com/products/crunchy-breaded-fish-sticks/

## Result from Joe (Oct 11, 2026)
Oven Air Fry Chef recorded this as "ground truth from Joe":
> Samsung Air Fry on bare rack runs much hotter/faster than package bake; the 0.96 time ratio and same-temp assumption failed for pizza.

Joe also questioned the same-temperature assumption: "I'm honestly most confused about why you use the bake temp for air fry more than anything. That wont get me my crispy pizza at all". Oven Air Fry Chef replied that Samsung's guide lists some foods 25°F above the package temperature, such as tater tots. Nothing has been re-tested since.
