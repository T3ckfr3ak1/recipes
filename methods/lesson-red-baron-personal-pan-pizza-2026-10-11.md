# Lesson learned: Red Baron Personal Pan Pizza burned in Samsung Air Fry (Oct 11, 2026)

- **Sources:** Joe, Grok Bot and Oven Air Fry Chef chats, Sun Oct 11, 2026, 4:29 to 5:13 PM CDT.
- **Joe's instruction (5:13 PM):** "Don't bother giving me the right directions. Whats done is done". No corrected air fry directions were made, and none are given here.

## What the box says (Joe's photo of the package, sent to Oven Air Fry Chef)
Conventional oven (recommended):
1. "Preheat oven to 375°F. Place oven rack in center position."
2. "Remove pizza from clear plastic wrap. Discard plastic and silver cooking tray. Caution: do not use tray in oven."
3. "When oven is preheated, place frozen pizza on a cookie sheet and then place on the center rack. Bake for 22-24 minutes or until cheese is melted and lightly browned."

Microwave oven (1100 watts):
- "Only cook 1 pizza at a time."
- Remove the overwrap and place the frozen pizza on the silver cooking surface, on a microwave-safe plate.
- "Cook on high (100% power) for 2 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes 45 seconds." It's ready when the cheese is completely melted. Let it cool 1 minute.

## What happened
1. **Grok Bot's first answer** (made up, no research): Air Fry 400°F, 16 to 20 min, "in its paper-thin foil pan". Joe rejected it: "What do you mean by "in its pan" that doesn't exist".
2. **Oven Air Fry Chef, from web research:** Bake 400°F preheat, then Air Fry 400°F on rack 3, 18 to 23 min. Later it changed to 400°F for 25 min using the 0.96 time ratio. The web research used Red Baron's hand-tossed page (400°F, 25 to 27 min), not this pizza's box.
3. **Joe:** "You're shitty instructions warnt even based on the actual instructions fornthe food". He sent the box photo.
4. **Oven Air Fry Chef corrected to the box:** Bake 375°F to preheat, cancel, then Air Fry 375°F for **22 min**, directly on the center rack, with the silver tray removed.
5. **Result (Joe, 5:12 PM):** "You burnt my fucking pizza". Joe's photo showed a dark, charred top and edges. Joe later added: "The crust is only a little overdone".

## Lessons the bots recorded
- Always use the package directions from Joe's photo when he supplies one, not web guesses (Oven Air Fry Chef memory).
- Samsung Air Fry on the bare rack ran much hotter or faster than the package bake directions. The 0.96 time ratio and the same-temperature assumption failed for pizza. Cheese and toppings on a thin crust scorch sooner than fries and tots (Oven Air Fry Chef).
- The meal pack (Oct 20) says: "Use regular Bake mode for frozen pizza (Air Fry mode can burn the top before the crust is done)."
- Joe's rules from that session: no estimates or hedged ranges; no confidence footnotes after instructions; don't list sources unless he asks. See `oven-air-fry-chef-answer-rules.md`.
