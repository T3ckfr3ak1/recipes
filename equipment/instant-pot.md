# Instant Pot

- **Model:** Emeril Lagasse Pressure AirFryer, 8 quart, model Y8D-AF-36 (Joe, Oct 24, 2026). Owner's manual: `appliance-manuals/emeril-lagasse-y8d-af-36.pdf`. That manual covers this 8-quart model and the 6-quart Y6D-AF-36.
- **Functions (Joe, Oct 23, 2026):** pressure cook, sous vide, steam, saute, slow cook, air fry, canning, yogurt.
- **Lids Joe owns (Joe, Oct 23, 2026, glass lid identified Oct 24, 2026):** pressure cooker lid, air fryer lid, and one glass lid from his stainless pots and pans. That lid has a stainless rim and a stainless handle, and the same style of lid fits those pots and pans. It fits this pot snugly. It is not the official Instant Pot glass lid. It has no vent, so it holds heat and moisture like a standard crock-pot lid but doesn't hold pressure. The Oct 23 note called this lid a KitchenAid metal-rimmed glass lid.
- **Inner pot (Joe, Oct 23, 2026):** Joe threw away the original nonstick (Teflon) pot and replaced it with an OEM stainless steel inner pot that has a thick bottom. All of his cooks from Oct 23 on use the stainless pot. Same cookware rule as the rest of his kitchen (Joe, Oct 24, 2026): stainless or cast iron, and he is staying with them.
- **Air frying (Joe, Oct 23, 2026):** Joe owns the air fryer lid and a fryer basket for this pot. Right now he air fries in his Samsung oven's Air Fry mode (see `equipment/samsung-oven-air-fry.md`), but he may start air frying in this pot. Ask before planning Instant Pot air fry steps.
- **Functions Joe uses (Joe, to Thrifty, Oct 20, 2026):** "my insta pot can pressure or crok". That means pressure cook or slow cook.
- **Lid:** The slow-cook glass lid is the stainless-set lid in the list above. It has no vent and does not hold pressure.
- **Slow cook temperature control (Joe, Oct 23, 2026):** slow cook mode takes a set temperature in Fahrenheit. He has used 195F and 212F. The timer only starts counting once the pot reaches the set point.
- **How Joe wants recipes written for this pot (Joe, Oct 23, 2026):** exactly one cook time and one Fahrenheit temperature for the whole cook. No Low/Medium/High wording, no temperature ranges, no doneness ranges in the recipe.
- **Heating behavior (Head Chef research, Oct 23, 2026):** multicookers heat only from the bottom through a thin pot, so slow cooking comes up to temp slower than a ceramic crock. America's Test Kitchen found most multicookers slow cook too cool (https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/2242-multicookers). Instant Pot's own guide: https://www.instantpot.be/en/blog-en/informationen-en/how-the-slow-cooker-function-works-on-instant-pot-multicookers-model-guide/
- **Known results:** see `methods/lesson-instant-pot-pulled-pork-2026-10-23.md`.
- **Modes used in the meal pack recipes:** Saute (searing), Pressure Cook on High with natural release, and Slow Cook (Low or High). Recipes R3 and R7.
- **Meal pack tips:**
  - "Don't fill past the 2/3 line."
  - Scrape the bottom after adding liquid "(this prevents the "Burn" warning)".
  - "Put the trivet in if you have it". Whether Joe has a trivet is not recorded.
