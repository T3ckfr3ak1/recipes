# Samsung gas range NX60A6511SS/AA

- **Model:** Samsung freestanding gas range NX60A6511SS/AA (Joe, Oct 24, 2026). Fingerprint-resistant stainless, 6.0 cu. ft. Five burners: right front 18,000 BTU dual ring, left front 17,000 BTU, right rear 5,000 BTU, left rear 9,500 BTU, center oval 10,000 BTU. Oven modes include Bake, Convection Bake, Convection Roast, Broil (low/high), Air Fry, Bread Proof, and Dehydrate. The Air Fry tray is sold separately. Owner's manual: `appliance-manuals/samsung-nx60a6511ss.pdf`.
- **What Joe described (Oct 24, 2026):** Samsung stainless, all gas, matching the ME21M706BAS microwave. Five-burner cooktop with a center oval burner. Oven modes he named: Air Fry, Dehydrate, Bake, Convection Bake, and Broil. This file is that range.
- **What it is (Joe, to Thrifty, Oct 20, 2026):** "i dont have an air fryer, i have a samsung oven with an air fry feature but its somewhere between air frying and baking"
- **Modes used in Joe's recipes:** Bake and Air Fry. The meal pack uses regular Bake mode for frozen pizza, potatoes (Air Fry also works) and the pork chop reverse sear, and Air Fry mode for most other oven steps.

## Joe's verified rule (Joe, to Grok Bot, Oct 11, 2026)
> Had research been done the bot would know the instructions would start with preheat by setting bake to 400. Once oven is preheated canel bake and airnfry at 400. Since the oven dosent preheat in air fry mode, but air fry works better if oven is preheated

How Oven Air Fry Chef applies it (from its bot profile): "(1) Preheat by setting regular BAKE to the target temperature (usually the same temp as the Air Fry step). (2) When the oven reports it is preheated, cancel Bake. (3) Start Air Fry at the target temperature and cook for the time given."

Joe cooks food directly on the rack unless the food needs a pan or tray (Joe's rule 2 for Oven Air Fry Chef, Oct 11, 2026).

## Samsung's official Air Fry guidance (researched by Oven Air Fry Chef, Oct 11, 2026)
- "Use the tray on rack position 3 with a baking sheet below. Preheating isn't needed. The range is 350–500°F and the default is 425°F. Air Fry is meant for a single rack."
- Samsung's frozen-food cooking guide lists 400–450°F. It has no pizza row.
- Samsung's separate Pizza mode defaults to 400°F on rack 3 or 4.
- From Samsung Members: an Air Fry tray is needed (it can be bought if the model didn't come with one), plus a baking sheet to catch drips.
- This model's manual says Air Fry is 350–500°F, preheating is not necessary, and the tray goes on rack 3 with a sheet underneath. Joe's preheat rule above still applies. The spec sheet says the tray is sold separately.

Sources: https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10006916/ , https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS00060981/ , https://r1.community.samsung.com/t5/others/air-fryer-oven/td-p/15035100 , https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/874945/samsung-30-inch-freestanding-electric-smart-range-owner-manual.html?page=42

## Known quirks
- Joe's own result (Oct 11, 2026): on the bare rack in Air Fry mode, a Red Baron Personal Pan Pizza burned at 375°F for 22 min. See `methods/lesson-red-baron-personal-pan-pizza-2026-10-11.md`.
- Meal pack note: "It cooks somewhere between a real air fryer and regular baking, so these times are a starting point." It also says "Tops brown faster than bottoms" and "this mode browns fast". See `methods/samsung-air-fry-mode-meal-pack-guide.md`.
- Users on Reddit and Samsung Members report that Samsung Air Fry runs slower than a countertop air fryer, so countertop times don't carry over. Users also report weak results without the tray (Oven Air Fry Chef research, Oct 11, 2026).
