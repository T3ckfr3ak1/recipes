# Measuring cups and food storage

**Source:** Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026.

## Measuring
- Pyrex glass measuring cups: two 1-cup, one 2-cup, one 4-cup.
- A small measure marked from 1 to 10 Tbsp (down to 1/2 oz).

## Storage
- Quart freezer bags and sandwich-size freezer bags.
- **No aluminum foil.** It's on the shopping list. Until Joe buys it, no recipe or plan step should use foil.
- Work meals go in quart Ziploc freezer bags, never Tupperware. See `methods/work-meal-packing.md`.

## Rule
- Use as few dishes as possible. Example: combine and microwave the filling right in the 4-cup Pyrex (Pork and Black Bean Burritos, `recipes/e48582ad361a.json`).
