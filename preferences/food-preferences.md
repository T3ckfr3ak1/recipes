# Joe's food preferences and brand rules

**Sources:** Joe's messages to Thrifty (Oct 20, 2026), Thrifty's memory, and Meal Plan's bot profile.

## Won't eat or limits
- Won't eat: pickles, mushrooms, zucchini, asparagus, cantaloupe, cucumbers.
- Not a fan of rice. Joe: "rice isnt out, but i dont cook with it myself much".
- Keeps tomatoes light because of acid reflux. Joe: "i avoid tomatoes - acid reflux, i eat them, but not often".

## Likes
- Pizza (Red Baron personal pizzas), burgers, steak, hot wings.
- Pulled or shredded pork and chicken from the Instant Pot.
- Tyson BBQ chicken chunks, though they're pricey.
- Deli sandwiches, for work lunches only, not dinner or at home. Joe: "lunch not dinner, i could eat those every day".
- Snacks: Utz Pub Mix and Reese's.
- Drinks: Baja Blast, and at least one non-diet Monster a day.
- Candied jalapenos: "the jalepenos are good for lunch sandwiches".
- Peppers: Joe finds a blend of peppers makes the flavor flow better than a single pepper type (example: candied jalapenos plus El Yucateco XXXtra Hot habanero in the Oct 23, 2026 pulled pork). He plans to start making his own seasoning blends again.

## Storage rules
- Sour cream (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): opened sour cream doesn't last, and it doesn't freeze well. Don't plan freezer meals with sour cream inside; add it at serving.
- Candied jalapenos (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): 'to taste' applies only to toppings (burritos, sandwiches, bowls). Cooked meats get an exact amount scaled from the pulled pork ratio (1/2 cup peppers, no juice, per 8.2 lb). His kids eat jalapenos; only habanero is Joe and Warren only.
- Seasoning (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): every cook step must include its seasoning. Plain, unseasoned meat is never acceptable.
- No foil until Joe buys it. Work meals go in quart Ziploc freezer bags, never Tupperware.

## Household
- As of Oct 24, 2026 Joe is single (he and his girlfriend broke up). Meals are for Joe alone or Joe plus his three sons.
- Thursday dinners with his kids are Jack's pizzas. Joe: "what the kids will and wont eat is such a pain in the ass".
