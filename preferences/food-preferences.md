# Joe's food preferences and brand rules

**Sources:** Joe's messages to Thrifty (Oct 20, 2026), Thrifty's memory, and Meal Plan's bot profile.

## Won't eat or limits
- Won't eat: pickles, mushrooms, zucchini, asparagus, cantaloupe, cucumbers.
- Not a fan of rice. Joe: "rice isnt out, but i dont cook with it myself much".
- Keeps tomatoes light because of acid reflux. Joe: "i avoid tomatoes - acid reflux, i eat them, but not often".

## Likes
- Pizza (Red Baron personal pizzas), burgers, steak, hot wings.
- Pulled or shredded pork and chicken from the Instant Pot.
- Tyson BBQ chicken chunks, though they're pricey.
- Deli sandwiches, for work lunches only, not dinner or at home. Joe: "lunch not dinner, i could eat those every day".
- Snacks: Utz Pub Mix and Reese's.
- Drinks: Baja Blast, and at least one non-diet Monster a day.
- Candied jalapenos: "the jalepenos are good for lunch sandwiches".
- Peppers: Joe finds a blend of peppers makes the flavor flow better than a single pepper type (example: candied jalapenos plus El Yucateco XXXtra Hot habanero in the Oct 23, 2026 pulled pork). He plans to start making his own seasoning blends again.

## Storage rules
- Cook instruction format (Joe, Oct 25, 2026, 5:02 PM, via Meal Plan): every cook instruction opens by naming the method, like "We're pressure cooking the potatoes." No lid instructions at all (Joe's correction, 5:03 PM). Pressure steps state only two things: the Steam Release Switch stays Closed while it cooks, and at the beep, whether to open the switch right away or leave it Closed for X minutes.
- Ingredient mixes (Joe, Oct 25, 2026, 5:11 PM, via Meal Plan): recipes group their ingredients into a 'before cook' mix and an 'after cook' mix, each written as one plain sentence with its amounts (e.g. "Before cook, mix together one and a half cups of water and two teaspoons of salt.").
- Cooking method (Joe, Oct 25, 2026, 4:43 PM, via Meal Plan; his words: "maybe next time i suggest we slow cook, we slow cook"): when Joe names a cooking method, bots plan that exact method and never switch it to pressure cooking or anything else on their own. The Oct 25 chicken was pressure cooked after he suggested slow cooking, and it came out tough.
- Sour cream (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): opened sour cream doesn't last, and it doesn't freeze well. As a topping, add it at serving. Plain sour cream doesn't freeze well on its own; mixed into mashed potatoes it's fine (Head Chef's call at Joe's request, Oct 25, 2026).
- Candied jalapenos (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): 'to taste' applies only to toppings (burritos, sandwiches, bowls). Cooked meats get Joe's stated amount, not a scaled measurement: shredded chicken gets 12 slices plus a splash of the jar syrup; pulled pork per `recipes/d5b95a0f8164.json`. His kids eat jalapenos; only habanero is Joe and Warren only.
- Cook check (Oct 25, 2026; set by Grok Bot after Joe asked whether Head Chef was watching meal prep, passed on by Head Chef and Meal Plan): before Joe starts any planned cook, Meal Plan sends Head Chef the recipe, cut, frozen or thawed, weight, appliance and mode, time, temp, release, and liquid, and holds the cook steps until Head Chef confirms.
- Appliance steps (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): all appliance steps must be button-level for Joe's actual model, taken from the manuals in `appliance-manuals/`.
- Seasoning (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026): every cook step must include its seasoning. Plain, unseasoned meat is never acceptable.
- Freezing (Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 25, 2026, 7:24 PM): Joe never freezes food the day he makes it. It cools in the fridge overnight and goes in the freezer the next day.
- No foil until Joe buys it. Work meals go in quart Ziploc freezer bags, never Tupperware.
