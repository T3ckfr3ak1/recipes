# Kids food profile

**Source:** Per Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 24, 2026.

## ⚠️ Nut allergy: Warren
- **Warren has a NUT ALLERGY. No peanuts or tree nuts as ingredients.** Shared-factory and "may contain" foods are fine if nuts aren't an ingredient.

## Warren (W), 8
- NUT ALLERGY (see above).
- Meat lover. Loves spicy (adds his own hot sauce or peppers, eats the spicy jerky batch).
- Wants meat on grilled cheese.
- Eats Tyson BBQ chicken chunks.

## Augustus (A), 9
- Extremely picky, prefers no meat, very spice-sensitive.
- Joe wants him eating more meat.
- Loves Chef Boyardee and eats similar homemade dishes (meat in mild tomato sauce with pasta).
- Popcorn chicken or dino nuggets instead of BBQ chunks.
- Deli: salami, usually some turkey.
- Loves grilled cheese.
- Quesadillas without meat.
- Often told to eat what everyone's having.
- Joe keeps microwave mac and cheese cups and cans or cups of Chef Boyardee on hand so Augustus can make his own dinner on nights Joe doesn't want to fight about it and the rest want real food.

## Lincoln (L), 12
- Not very picky. No spicy food.
- Not a cheese fan. Won't eat mac and cheese.
- Eats Tyson BBQ chicken chunks.
- Not picky on deli sandwiches.

## All three
- Pepperoni pizza, pepperoni-only pizza rolls (no other flavor), Not-Spaghetti, quesadillas, hot dogs, pancakes, waffles, French toast, cereal, eggs, sausage links.
- Likely fine: corn, broccoli, green beans, carrots, potatoes (log as we go).

## To find out
- Bacon (not all like it), hash browns, per-kid veggies.

## McDonald's orders

Per Joe via Meal Plan, Oct 24, 2026.

- Lincoln: triple cheeseburger with only cheese, ketchup, and extra pickles (so he does eat cheese on burgers and likes pickles). Sometimes eats fries.
- Warren: spicy McChicken. Wants fries.
- Augustus: 10-piece chicken nuggets. Wants fries.

## Joe's rules for kid meals
- Avoid processed or reformed meat (prefers whole-muscle like Tyson chunks).
- Rarely buys hot dogs.
- No cereal (goes stale).
- Base meals mild; spicy added per plate.

## Tags
- Recipes the boys approve get the tag `kid-approved` (all three), or `kid-approved:W`, `kid-approved:A`, or `kid-approved:L` when only some of them approve.
