- Pork chops are already in the fridge. Cook them tomorrow.

### Sun Oct 25: Prep day for the Mon to Thu work week
1. Cook all 5 pork chops (R17). Eat 1 for dinner.
2. Roast a 2 lb batch of diced russets. **Roasted diced potatoes (from Oven Air Fry Chef, not yet tested by Joe).** Sat Oct 24 and Sun Nov 1 use these same steps.
   - Cook:
     1. Dice the russets 3/4 inch and pat them dry. Toss with oil and seasoning.
     2. Spread on a lightly oiled half sheet pan. Max 2 lb per batch. Rack position 3.
     3. Preheat with Bake 425F. When preheated, cancel Bake.
     4. Air Fry 425F for 25 min. Flip and stir at 12 min.
     5. Done when fork-tender and the edges are golden. Add 3 min if needed.
   - Reheat:
     1. Preheat with Bake 400F. When preheated, cancel Bake.
     2. Air Fry 400F for 8 min, stirring at 4 min, to 165F.
     3. The weeknight containers can be microwaved instead (see Mon Oct 26 to Thu Oct 29).
3. Cook corn and broccoli (R16).
4. Build 4 meal containers, each with 1 chop, roasted potatoes, and a veg. Label them Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu.
5. Mon and Tue go in the fridge. Wed and Thu go in the freezer.
6. Pack the Mon to Wed deli sandwiches (follow the R19 pack-ahead tips). Thu's and Fri's get packed Wed night (packing isn't cooking).

### Sun Nov 1: Prep day for the Nov 2 to 4 work week
1. Cook the 3-cheese chicken skillet bag meal (R24, follow the bag). Cool and put it in a container for Mon.
2. Make the BBQ pulled pork quesadillas from the "Nov 3 BBQ quesadillas" pork bag (R5: 2 tortillas, Honey BBQ, light mozzarella). Cool, wrap, fridge for Tue.
3. Roast about 1/2 lb diced russets (1 medium potato) for the Nov 4 bowl (steps under "Sun Oct 25: Prep day"). Portion the Nov 4 Buffalo chicken bowl (R23): the "Nov 4 Buffalo bowl" chicken tossed with Buffalo sauce (R8), stored with the roasted diced potatoes (or hash browns). Fridge for Wed.

## Storage, Freezing, and Reheating

| Food | Fridge | Freezer | Reheat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulled pork | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months, flat bags with juice | Thaw overnight. Microwave covered with a splash of juice, 50% power, stir every minute. Or skillet with a splash of water. |
| Shredded chicken | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months | Same as pork; add a splash of broth (Better Than Bouillon + water) so it stays moist. |
| Cooked pork chops | 3 to 4 days | Best within 1 month, wrapped individually | See R18. Low power, slow. |
| Meal containers (chop, roasted potatoes, veg) | 3 to 4 days (Mon and Tue containers) | Best within 1 month (Wed and Thu containers); thaw in the fridge the night before | Chop per R18, low power, slow. Potatoes and veg in the microwave, covered. |
| Roasted diced potatoes | Up to 3 days | In the meal containers only | Preheat with Bake 400F, cancel, then Air Fry 400F for 8 min, stirring at 4, to 165F. The weeknight containers can be microwaved instead. |
| Cooked quesadillas (pork, BBQ pork) | 3 to 4 days, wrapped | 2 to 3 months, wrapped individually | Skillet over medium-low, or microwave then skillet to re-crisp. Frozen work lunches thaw in the work fridge or cooler. |
| Cooked skillet bag meal, Buffalo chicken bowl | 3 to 4 days | | Microwave covered, 50% power, stir halfway. |
| Tortellini alfredo | 3 days | Not recommended (sauce separates) | Microwave at 50% power, 1 min at a time, stirring, with a splash of milk. Or skillet over low with milk. |
| Cheesy garlic bread | 3 days, in foil | 1 month, in foil | Oven 350°F, in foil 8 min, then open the foil 2 to 3 min to re-crisp. Microwave makes it chewy. |
| Cooked wings (deli or homemade) | 3 to 4 days | 2 months | Air Fry mode 375°F, 8 to 12 min, flip once. |
| Cooked chicken breast | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months | Slice, microwave 50% power with a splash of broth, covered. |
| Hot deli food from the store | Eat within 2 hours of pickup or refrigerate. Use leftovers within 3 to 4 days. | | |
| Opened lunchmeat | 3 to 5 days | Unopened packs freeze 1 to 2 months | Thaw in the fridge. |
| Raw ground beef | 1 to 2 days | 3 to 4 months | Thaw in fridge the night before. |
| Raw chicken breasts (fresh) | 1 to 2 days (or by use-by date) | 9 months | Thaw in fridge 1 day. |

General rules: get cooked food into the fridge within 2 hours. Freeze flat in labeled bags (what + date). Thaw in the fridge, not on the counter. When in doubt, throw it out.
