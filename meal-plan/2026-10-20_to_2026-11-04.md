## Storage, Freezing, and Reheating

| Food | Fridge | Freezer | Reheat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulled pork | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months, flat bags with juice | Thaw overnight. Microwave covered with a splash of juice, 50% power, stir every minute. Or skillet with a splash of water. |
| Shredded chicken | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months | Same as pork; add a splash of broth (Better Than Bouillon + water) so it stays moist. |
| Cooked pork chops | 3 to 4 days | Best within 1 month, each in its own quart Ziploc freezer bag | See R18. Low power, slow. |
| Work bowls (mashed potatoes, shredded chicken, habanero jack, candied jalapenos; quart Ziplocs) | 3 to 4 days (Mon and Tue) | Wed and Thu; thaw in the fridge the night before | At work: out of the bag onto a paper plate in a 1 inch ring, 2 Tbsp liquid, damp paper towel. 1000 W at 50%: fridge-cold 4 + 4 min, partly frozen 5 + 5 min, stir between, stand 3 min, 165F. 700-900 W use 70%, 1100-1200 W use 40%. Full steps in `methods/work-meal-packing.md` (from Oven Air Fry Chef, not yet tested by Joe). |
| Pulled pork hash brown bowls (Nov 3, Nov 4) | Pork thawed in the fridge Nov 2 night; use within 3 to 4 days | Pork in the "Nov 3-4 bowls" bag until Nov 2 night; hash browns cooked Sun Nov 1, 2 quart-bag portions in the fridge | TBD (pending from Oven Air Fry Chef). |
| Pork and Black Bean Burritos | | Quart freezer bags; freezer time not specified by Joe | Out of the bag onto a paper plate, damp paper towel over it, microwave 2 min, flip, 2 min more, to 165F (starting point from the Pulled Pork Wrap, not tested). |
| Roasted diced potatoes | Up to 3 days | Not in this week's bowls (mashed instead) | Preheat with Bake 400F, cancel, then Air Fry 400F for 8 min, stirring at 4, to 165F. Or microwave. |
| Cooked quesadillas | 3 to 4 days, in a bag | 2 to 3 months, bagged individually | Skillet over medium-low, or microwave then skillet to re-crisp. Frozen work lunches thaw in the work fridge or cooler. |
| Cooked skillet bag meal | 3 to 4 days | | Microwave covered, 50% power, stir halfway. |
| Tortellini alfredo | 3 days | Not recommended (sauce separates) | Microwave at 50% power, 1 min at a time, stirring, with a splash of milk. Or skillet over low with milk. |
| Cheesy garlic bread | 3 days, in a quart Ziploc freezer bag | 1 month, in a quart Ziploc freezer bag | Oven 350°F 8 min, then 2 to 3 min more to re-crisp (uncovered, it may brown or dry faster). Microwave makes it chewy. |
| Cooked wings (deli or homemade) | 3 to 4 days | 2 months | Air Fry mode 375°F, 8 to 12 min, flip once. |
| Cooked chicken breast | 3 to 4 days | 2 to 3 months | Slice, microwave 50% power with a splash of broth, covered. |
| Hot deli food from the store | Eat within 2 hours of pickup or refrigerate. Use leftovers within 3 to 4 days. | | |
| Opened lunchmeat | 3 to 5 days | Unopened packs freeze 1 to 2 months | Thaw in the fridge. |
| Raw ground beef | 1 to 2 days | 3 to 4 months | Thaw in fridge the night before. |
| Raw chicken breasts (fresh) | 1 to 2 days (or by use-by date) | 9 months | Thaw in fridge 1 day. |

General rules: get cooked food into the fridge within 2 hours. Joe's rule: never freeze food the day it's made; it cools in the fridge overnight and goes in the freezer the next day. Freeze flat in labeled bags (what + date). Thaw in the fridge, not on the counter. When in doubt, throw it out.
